Thank you for buying Duesko Pro!

Your license key is on the order page and in the receipt email.

Activate Pro in three steps

  1. Copy your license key from the receipt email.
  2. Open Duesko's settings (Duesko → Settings, or ⌘,) and choose Pro.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. Everything unlocks right away.

Don't have Duesko yet?

Download Duesko for Mac. It needs macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Or download Duesko for Windows 10 or 11 (ARM version).

Using more than one Mac?

Your key works on up to 3 Macs. To move it to another Mac, click Deactivate on This Mac in Settings → Pro.

Need help?

Didn't get the email, or the key doesn't activate? Write to stepsergius@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
