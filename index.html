Your money stays your business.

Duesko never asks for a bank login and has no account. Your subscriptions live only on your Mac. No analytics, no tracking. It goes online only for exchange rates and service icons, which you can turn off, and to check a Pro key.

Questions

Does Duesko connect to my bank?

No. You add subscriptions yourself, which takes seconds since Duesko knows hundreds of services. Nothing about your accounts or cards ever leaves your Mac.

Is Duesko safe to install outside the App Store?

Yes. Duesko is signed with the developer's Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which checks it for malicious software before it's released. macOS opens it without warnings, and updates come from Duesko itself, signed the same way.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes, for Windows 10 and 11, on ordinary PCs and on ARM ones. It has the same screens, reminders and updates, and it opens backups and CSV files made by the Mac version. Two things are Mac-only for now: desktop widgets and live calendar sync (on Windows you can export a calendar file instead). The Windows installer isn't signed with a Microsoft certificate yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway. One Pro key works on up to 3 computers, Mac or Windows.

What happens when the free week ends?

Duesko keeps working for free with up to 5 subscriptions. Nothing you added is deleted, and reminders keep coming. Pro features are locked until you buy Pro.

Can I bring my subscriptions from another app?

Yes, with Pro: Duesko imports CSV files from other apps and lets you match their columns. Files exported from Duesko import in the free edition too.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Duesko is made for the Mac. You can show your payments in the Calendar app, which syncs them to your iPhone through iCloud or Google.

How do I activate Pro?

After you buy, the license key comes by email. Paste it in Duesko → Settings → Pro.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Duesko doesn't work for you, write within 14 days of purchase and you'll get your money back.
